What the labels mean

AI impact rating (0–100%)
How much of this skill's routine work today's AI can handle. It is a snapshot of current capability, not a prediction about your job.
Invest here (0–30%)
AI struggles with this. It needs judgement, relationships, or physical work that models can't replicate. This is where your career value compounds.
Partner with AI (31–60%)
AI does parts of this well but needs steering. Your edge is directing it and catching what it gets wrong.
Let AI handle (61–100%)
AI already does most of this. Automate it and move the time into your invest skills.
Role AI exposure
The average impact rating across every skill in the role. High exposure means the day-to-day changes a lot — not that the role disappears.
Confidence
How sure we are. Medium means supported by published research. Low means expert estimate. High means validated by practitioners — which is what your feedback unlocks.
